Compatibility

Built around M18.
Not every pack is the same.

PackGauge is designed for compatible Milwaukee M18 battery packs. It is not a universal battery tester. Hardware and firmware vary, so a family name or recognized type code cannot guarantee that every data block will be available.

Recognized does not mean fully verified.The type-code reference below describes recognized identities, not a guarantee that every pack or revision has been tested on PackGauge hardware.

01 / The scope

What the reader asks for.

The scanner iterates 184 register definitions. Core identity and five series cell-bank readings are broadly useful, while charge counts and times, discharge totals, fault counters, current/load histograms, and charge conditions appear only where the connected pack exposes them. Availability differs by pack firmware.

Capacity-based estimated equivalent cycles require a recognized nominal capacity and remain calculations—not direct measurements of remaining capacity.

03 / Missing history

Some Forge firmware reports less.

Forge firmware tested by the project exposes identity, cells, dates, and core charge/discharge totals, but lacks many fault, load-histogram, and charge-condition blocks at the known addresses. PackGauge reports those fields as unavailable instead of zero.

An unavailable field is not evidence that an event did not occur. This behavior is not a blanket statement about every Forge pack or firmware revision.

04 / The language

Three important distinctions.

Observed
Returned by the connected pack during this scan.
Calculated
Derived from observed values, such as five-bank voltage spread or estimated equivalent cycles.
Unavailable
The expected data block was not exposed. It is not converted to a reassuring zero.
