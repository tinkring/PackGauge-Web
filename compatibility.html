Compatibility

Designed for supported 18V lithium-ion tool battery packs.

PackGauge is not a universal battery tester. Hardware and firmware revisions vary, so visual similarity alone cannot guarantee communication or data availability.

01

What the scanner asks for.

The scanner requests identity, cell-bank readings, and other diagnostic records through the supported pack interface. Charge counts and times, discharge totals, fault counters, current/load histograms, and charge conditions are only shown where the connected pack exposes them.

Missing data stays unavailable. It is not turned into a reassuring zero.

02

Revision matters.

Different internal revisions can exist inside packs that look nearly identical from the outside. Connector behavior, readable addresses, and recorded history can differ by firmware and electronics revision.

Compatibility therefore means “known to communicate with the PackGauge interface,” not “every data field is guaranteed to exist.”

03

Newer firmware may expose less history.

Some supported packs provide core identity, cell-bank readings, dates, and high-level charge/discharge totals while omitting older fault, load-histogram, or charge-condition blocks.

An unavailable field is not evidence that an event did not occur.

04

How to read the language.

Observed
Returned by the connected pack during a scan.
Calculated
Derived from observed values, such as cell-bank voltage spread.
Unavailable
The expected data block was not exposed; it is not replaced with zero.

Compatibility does not imply safety, authentication, capacity, health, warranty status, or fitness for use. Review the safety and diagnostic limits before scanning.
