How it works

Connect. Scan. Understand.

  1. Connect.

    Provide USB-C power, then attach a compatible pack through the purpose-built signal interface.

  2. Scan.

    The read-oriented workflow iterates 184 register definitions. The pack may refresh its own recorded statistics during scanning.

  3. Understand.

    Review observed readings, calculated spread, and available history. Save CSV data to micro-SD for later comparison.

For compatible M18 packs.
Built to reveal, not reassure.

PackGauge is designed for compatible Milwaukee M18 battery packs. A scan is diagnostic evidence; it does not establish battery safety, authenticity, capacity, or health.
